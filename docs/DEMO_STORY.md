# GiftGuard: the demonstration that earns trust

The differentiator is visible work in a natural conversation. Voice is the entry point; the payoff is a donor found, a clear reason, a usable message and a reversible decision. Avoid presenting a generic chatbot or spending demo time on settings.

## Opening line

“Denise has a few hours a week to raise money. She should be talking to people, not hunting through spreadsheets.”

## Live sequence (synthetic data, November 7, 2026)

1. Click **Talk to GiftGuard** once. Say: “Help me get ready for Give Miami Day.” The agent reads the current workspace and offers one next step.
2. Say: “Show me Miguel Santos. Why does he need a call?” His record opens while the conversation stays live. The record establishes a $75 monthly pattern and a November 2 expected gift, five days late. A missing gift is a reason to check, not proof of a failed card.
3. Say: “Draft a gentle note for him in Spanish.” The actual returned draft appears on screen and can be copied. This is the practical payoff for a bilingual Miami nonprofit. Do not claim the SDK Note Writer ran if the draft is labeled a template.
4. Interrupt or redirect: “Now show me Rosa’s possible duplicates.” The agent navigates to the review, explains the supporting evidence and asks for staff judgment.
5. Ask to combine Rosa’s records. The exact review card appears. Click Confirm. The agent receives the server’s completion receipt and acknowledges it aloud. Open the history to show every transaction remains.
6. Use Undo and confirm it. The agent acknowledges the actual server outcome. Nothing needs to be taken on trust.

## Three moments to emphasize

- **It follows the work:** open donor details, change screens, and keep speaking without reconnecting.
- **It produces something useful:** a grounded explanation and a copyable personal note, not just an answer.
- **It shows its limits:** asks which donor when ambiguous, pauses the microphone for an approval, and cannot act on spoken “yes.”

## Keep the pitch honest

These are demonstrations of capability, not measured claims about money recovered, time saved or uniqueness among competitors. The local source and mocked protocol tests pass; the actual browser-to-OpenAI session, microphone, network latency and model access must be rehearsed on the unrestricted demo machine. Use only the made-up dataset. Prepare a backup screen recording only after a real successful rehearsal.

## Visible agent movement

Leave **Show activity** enabled during the live demonstration. The GiftGuard pointer follows actual tool activity to the relevant view, donor record, draft or approval card. Let the movement explain what the agent is doing while it speaks. It is a visual guide, not a simulated mouse: no automatic clicking, fabricated percentages or artificial pauses. The user keeps their own cursor and control. Reduced-motion mode and the off toggle remain available.
